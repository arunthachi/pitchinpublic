# Tiger Shark Tank Pilot Evidence

Last updated: 2026-08-12

Source: customer discovery meeting with a prospective organizing team.

## Confirmed needs

- Offer Pitch in Public as an optional practice resource that supports, rather than replaces, the organizer's in-person selection and event process.
- Let the organizer adapt the Pitch Standard to the structure already used in its application form.
- Give inexperienced and nontechnical founders step-by-step prompts before they record.
- Standardize the information founders present so judges can compare pitches more consistently without pretending that judgment is fully objective.
- Allow one event administrator to coordinate founder and organizing-team invitations.
- Let founders practice before mentors are recruited. For this pilot, mentor feedback may begin only after the organizer selects finalists.
- Support ideal-pitch examples and reference materials in a later, separately reviewed increment.

## Explicit boundaries

- Live streaming is not part of the practice product or this release. The organizer prioritizes attendance at the physical event.
- The current pilot uses human mentorship. AI guidance is a planned direction and must be labeled with model/source provenance when introduced.
- Participation may be optional for the program, but once a founder chooses event practice, each event take follows that event's published Pitch Standard.

## Implementation implications

- Keep event basics short, then make the editable Pitch Standard the organizer's clear next step.
- Do not require reviewer supply, assignments, or completed feedback before a founder can prepare a plan or record a take.
- Use plain language and progressive disclosure; do not assume startup, technical, or investor vocabulary.
- Keep the founder's Pitch plan visible before recording and available during preparation, preview, and details entry.
- Preserve immutable standard-version binding on every event take so later human or AI feedback is grounded in the criteria the founder actually followed.

## Pilot validation

In addition to product-wide improvement metrics, observe:

- Whether a nontechnical founder can prepare and record without live assistance.
- Whether organizers can translate their existing application into a useful Pitch Standard.
- Whether finalists receive timely mentor feedback after the organizer opens the review stage.
- Whether judges report that standardized inputs make comparison clearer and more consistent.
